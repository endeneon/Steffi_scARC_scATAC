The reference-based pipeline uses the following dataset. **Note: the original
marker-based annotation (Package A) did NOT use any reference dataset.**

> Lu Y, Yang A, Quan C, Pan Y, Zhang H, Li Y, Gao C, Lu H, et al.
> **A single-cell atlas of the multicellular ecosystem of primary and
> metastatic hepatocellular carcinoma.**
> *Nature Communications* 2022;13:4594.
> doi:[10.1038/s41467-022-32283-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32283-3)
> Data: GEO [GSE149614](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE149614)
> (71,915 cells; 10 patients; non-tumor liver / primary tumor / PVTT /
> metastatic lymph node)

## Marker-panel literature (Package A)

The 15 canonical lineage marker sets are standard HCC-TME lineage markers as
used in published human liver / HCC single-cell atlases:

- MacParland SA, et al. **Single cell RNA sequencing of human liver reveals
  distinct intrahepatic macrophage populations.** *Nat Commun* 2018;9:4383.
  doi:10.1038/s41467-018-06318-7
- Sharma A, et al. **Onco-fetal reprogramming of endothelial cells drives
  immunosuppressive macrophages in hepatocellular carcinoma.** *Cell*
  2020;183:377–394.e21. doi:10.1016/j.cell.2020.08.040
- Lu Y, et al. *Nat Commun* 2022;13:4594 (above) — HCC TME lineage markers.

Malignant-hepatocyte panel genes (AFP, GPC3, MDK, TOP2A, IGF2BP1, FOXM1,
PEG10, BIRC5, SPP1) are established HCC tumor/oncofetal markers; the mature
hepatocyte panel (ALB, HNF4A, CYP3A4, CYP2E1, TF, APOA1, CPS1, SERPINA1)
comprises canonical liver-function genes.
